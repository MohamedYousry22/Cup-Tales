Cup Tales Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Information we collect

We collect the name, email address and phone number you provide, saved delivery addresses, cart and order details, and identifiers needed for authentication and push notifications.

How we use information

We use this information to manage your account, prepare and deliver orders, contact you about an order, remember your preferences, prevent abuse, and send order updates when notifications are enabled.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication and database services, Firebase and Google for Android services and Google Sign-In, and OneSignal for push notifications. These providers process information needed to provide their services.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. You may permanently delete your account and associated profile, addresses, cart and order history from Profile → Delete account. You may also review our account deletion instructions.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and addresses, disable notifications, sign out, or delete your account. System notification permission can also be changed in device settings.

Security

We use authenticated connections, database access controls and row-level security. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to what is needed to operate Cup Tales.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions, email m1662006@gmail.com.
